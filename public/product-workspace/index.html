Research notes · source links

Large-format printing, tooling & casting

Research-backed route selection for products ranging from wearable devices to chairs, printed molds, patterns and cast parts.

RESEARCHED · 2026-11-14
Key distinctionBuild envelope describes size, not print speed, throughput, cost or structural suitability. “A large 3D printer” can refer to different processes. Large-format polymer FFF prints polymer parts; polymer-printed tooling can form a mold or lay-up tool; foundry sand binder jetting directly prints sand molds and cores; investment-casting workflows may print a pattern that is later used to make a casting. These process routes need separate material, machine, post-processing and validation records.

Large-format polymer FFF

Direct-printed parts, prototypes and tooling

ROUTE REFERENCE

BigRep’s ONE.5 brochure lists FFF/FDM technology and a nominal 1005 × 1005 × 1005 mm build volume. Treat that as a machine-specific envelope, not a guarantee that every part fits or is buildable; collect actual machine, material, nozzle, orientation and build-margin values for each project.

BigRep ONE.5 technical brochure ↗

The template includes part and available machine envelope fields plus process route, material, build stages and validation records.

Furniture & chair examples

Full-size or custom product candidates

EXAMPLE · NOT A LOAD RATING

BigRep describes furniture makers using large-format printing for customized end-use furnishings. A chair workspace should separately record intended user/load, support geometry, joints, material/process, orientation and a test plan; the cited example does not qualify a new chair design.

RH-Engineering / manoFigura furniture case ↗

Use the furniture template to capture application, load requirement, machine envelope, material, assembly, inspection and pass/fail evidence.

Printed sand molds & cores

Dedicated foundry binder-jet route

CASTING ROUTE

ExOne describes CAD-to-sand-print workflows for casting molds and cores. Its VX4000 is listed with a 4 × 2 × 1 m continuous build envelope. This is specialized sand binder jetting; a filament printer’s volume alone does not make it a foundry sand printer.

ExOne sand casting molds and cores ↗

The casting template prompts for cast material, core/mold geometry, draft/parting, vents, gating, binder/sand, post-process, foundry constraints and trial-cast checks.

Oversize parts & segmentation

Design before splitting the model

PROPOSED WORKFLOW

For oversized polymer parts, capture orientation and machine capacity first. If segmentation is needed, record joint geometry, alignment features, load path, assembly process, tolerances and a joint coupon. Large format can reduce some segmentation needs, but fit, strength and finishing remain project-specific.

BigRep guide to oversized parts and joints ↗

Dimensional and part qualification

Measurement and test records belong with the product

VALIDATION REQUIRED

NIST’s additive manufacturing qualification work highlights measurement challenges for complex geometry, defects and anisotropic mechanical behavior. For ordinary projects, use a proportionate, documented plan; safety-critical and regulated products require applicable standards and qualified engineering review.
